A new look for a new era

KOZI is a clothing and eyewear label from Angeles City. We pair sculpted metal frames with graffiti-printed tees, inspired by early-2000s chrome and made in small batches.

Our mission

Make bold self-expression feel effortless. Every frame is designed in-house and every drop is limited, so what you wear is yours alone.

Our vision

To be the chrome-and-streetwear label that Central Luzon is known for, and to put KOZI on the faces of a creative generation across the Philippines and beyond.

The crew

The people who wear KOZI before anyone else — stylists, riders, skaters, and night-shift creatives. Chrome on the eyes, grit in the seams. Every frame and every drop is worn first by the crew, then sent into the world.

01

Made in small batches

Every drop is numbered. We cast the frames, print the tees, and cut the leather in limited runs — so what you wear stays rare.

02

Chrome on the eyes

Our design language comes from early-2000s metal and light. Sculpted silhouettes, welded details, and lenses that catch the street.

03

Worn by the crew

Before a piece goes live, it's tested on real nights — clubs, rides, rooftops. If it doesn't survive the crew, it doesn't ship.
